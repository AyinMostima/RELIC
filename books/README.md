# Methods of Ecological Analysis / 《生态学分析方法》

**Author / 作者:** Yuchong Long (隆宇翀)

This directory hosts the lightweight preview PDF editions of the bilingual theoretical and methodological monograph accompanying the **RELIC** (*Recomposable Ecosystem Library for Integrated Coupling*) framework:

- **English Edition:** *Methods of Ecological Analysis: Mathematical Foundations, Structure-Preserving Discretization, and Differentiable Earth System Coupling* (4,264 pages: Prologue + 6 Volumes, 30 Chapters, 169 Sections)
- **Chinese Edition (中文版):** 《生态学分析方法》（全书 3,700 页：序章 + 六卷 30 章、169 节）

> **Note:** Only compiled PDF editions are distributed. High-resolution 200-dpi reading editions and lossless 600-dpi print editions are attached as downloadable assets on **[GitHub Releases (`v1.0.0`)](https://github.com/AyinMostima/RELIC/releases/tag/v1.0.0)**.

## Six-Volume Architecture / 全书六卷三十章结构

1. **Prologue (序章):** Global Ecological Systems, Structure-Function Relations, and Five Discrepancy Channels ($E_1$--$E_5$)
2. **Volume I (Chapters 1--5 | 第一卷：账本、类型与有限维动力基础):** Conservation Ledgers, Scientific Quantity Type Quads/Quintuples, Real Analysis, Metzler/M-Matrices, Left-Nullspace Stoichiometry, and ODE Memory Kernels
3. **Volume II (Chapters 6--10 | 第二卷：测度、泛函与弯曲空间外微分):** Measure Theory, Jensen Curvature Gaps, Sobolev Spaces, Differential Forms, Generalized Stokes Theorem, Discrete Exterior Calculus (DEC), and Hodge Stars
4. **Volume III (Chapters 11--14 | 第三卷：代数范畴与热力学哈密顿几何):** Monoidal Categories, Typed Cochain Morphisms, Port-Hamiltonian Systems (IPHS), GENERIC Double Degeneracy, and Bounded Irreversible Hamiltonian-Stochastic Systems (BIHSS)
5. **Volume IV (Chapters 15--21 | 第四卷：五大圈层生物地球化学机理):** Atmosphere (Radiation, MOST Turbulence, Gas-Phase Photochemistry), Hydrosphere (Unified FVM, Richards Equation, Snow), Biosphere (FvCB Photosynthesis, Medlyn Stomata, Leaf-to-Canopy BVOC Synthesis), Pedosphere (Multi-Pool C-N-P, Freeze-Thaw ALE), and Anthroposphere
6. **Volume V (Chapters 22--26 | 第五卷：保结构跨尺度耦合、刚性分裂与随机过程):** Adjoint Conservative Remapping, Quotient-Fibre Closure Obstructions, Lie/Strang Operator Splitting, Saltation Matrices at Kinks, SDEs, Gillespie Jump Processes, and Structural Causal Models
7. **Volume VI (Chapters 27--30 | 第六卷：可辨识性、可微同化与 RELIC 治理架构):** Variable Projection (VarPro), Structural Identifiability, Discrete Adjoint Checkpointing, 3D-Var/EnKF/HMC Data Assimilation, GraphIR Compilation, Multi-Rate Savepoint Transactions, and L1--L4 / I1--I7 Verification
