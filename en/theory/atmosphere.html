Atmosphere Theory

Scope, process inventory, and certificate map

The RELIC atmosphere domain (relic.scientific.atmosphere) supplies radiative energy partitioning, Monin-Obukhov surface-layer turbulence, vertical and three-dimensional boundary-layer tracer transport, parallel dry-deposition resistance networks, open photostationary and closed constant-volume gas-phase chemistry, and exact-replay stochastic weather generators (Methods of Ecological Analysis, Chapters 17, 18, and 27). In addition, relic.coupling.observation provides read-only ScaleCouplingFunctor observation operators that project surface fluxes and layered mixing ratios onto satellite column retrievals and eddy-covariance tower supports.

Eleven concrete scientific Functors under relic.scientific.atmosphere carry twelve declarative IPHSEquivalenceCertificate files (eleven primary class certificates plus one secondary Level 5 constructor-conditioned certificate on ChemistryFunctor):

Solar, thermal, and canopy radiation

Surface radiative budget and spectral PAR partitioning

relic.scientific.atmosphere.radiation.RadiationFunctor (Level 2) evaluates downward and net shortwave and longwave irradiances [W m\(^{-2}\)] from potential insolation \(S_{\mathrm{pot}}\), cosine solar zenith angle \(\mu_0 = \cos\theta_z\), cloud fraction \(f_c \in [0, 1]\), air and surface temperatures \(T_a, T_s\) [K], relative humidity \(\mathrm{RH} \in [0, 100]\), surface albedo \(\alpha \in [0, 1]\), and surface emissivity \(\varepsilon_s \in (0, 1]\) (Methods of Ecological Analysis, Chapter 17):

\[ S_\downarrow = S_{\mathrm{pot}}\,\tau_c(f_c), \qquad S_{\mathrm{net}} = (1 - \alpha)\,S_\downarrow, \]
\[ L_\downarrow = \varepsilon_a(e_a, T_a, f_c)\,\sigma_{\mathrm{SB}} T_a^4, \qquad L_\uparrow = \varepsilon_s\,\sigma_{\mathrm{SB}} T_s^4 + (1 - \varepsilon_s)\,L_\downarrow, \]
\[ L_{\mathrm{net}} = L_\downarrow - L_\uparrow = \varepsilon_s\left(L_\downarrow - \sigma_{\mathrm{SB}} T_s^4\right), \qquad R_n = S_{\mathrm{net}} + L_{\mathrm{net}}, \]

where \(\sigma_{\mathrm{SB}}\) is fetched from relic.utils.physical_constants (get_physical_constant("stefan_boltzmann")) and clear-sky atmospheric emissivity follows Brutsaert (1975), \(\varepsilon_{a,\mathrm{clear}} = c_B\,(e_a / T_a)^{1/7}\), modulated by cloud cover. Photosynthetically active radiation (PAR) and photosynthetic photon flux density (\(\mathrm{PPFD}\), [\(\mu\)mol m\(^{-2}\) s\(^{-1}\)]) are split into direct-beam and diffuse components:

\[ I_{\mathrm{PAR}} = f_{\mathrm{PAR}}\,S_\downarrow, \qquad \mathrm{PPFD} = \kappa_{Q}\,I_{\mathrm{PAR}}, \qquad \mathrm{PPFD}_{\mathrm{diff}} = f_{\mathrm{diff}}(\mu_0, f_c)\,\mathrm{PPFD}, \qquad \mathrm{PPFD}_{\mathrm{dir}} = \mathrm{PPFD} - \mathrm{PPFD}_{\mathrm{diff}}. \]

When leaf_area_index (\(L_{\mathrm{AI}} \ge 0\)) is present, canopy interception follows Beer-Lambert extinction with direct and diffuse extinction coefficients \(k_b(\mu_0)\) and \(k_d\):

\[ \tau_{\mathrm{dir}} = \exp\!\left(-k_b(\mu_0)\,L_{\mathrm{AI}}\right), \qquad \tau_{\mathrm{diff}} = \exp\!\left(-k_d\,L_{\mathrm{AI}}\right), \qquad f_{\mathrm{PAR,abs}} = 1 - \frac{I_{\mathrm{PAR,dir}}\tau_{\mathrm{dir}} + I_{\mathrm{PAR,diff}}\tau_{\mathrm{diff}}}{\max(I_{\mathrm{PAR}}, \varepsilon_I)}. \]

Any positivity clamp applied to noisy shortwave or emissivity inputs is recorded explicitly in diagnostics.radiation.shortwave_clipping_residual and diagnostics.radiation.emissivity_clipping_residual.

Cochain radiation fields and radiative balance validation

relic.scientific.atmosphere.radiation.RadiationField (Level 3) treats gridded irradiance as a discrete scalar field on grid_coordinates, supporting bilinear or trilinear query projection onto source_coordinates and discrete gradient evaluation \(g_h = d_h I_h\) while reporting domain-clamp residuals in diagnostics.radiation_field.clipping_residual.

relic.scientific.atmosphere.radiation_energy_conservation.RadiationEnergyConservationValidator (Level 1) is a pure diagnostic observer (\(\Delta x_h = 0\)) verifying the pointwise radiative closure residual

\[ r_{\mathrm{rad}} = R_n - \left[(1 - \alpha)S_\downarrow + L_\downarrow - L_\uparrow\right] \]

against the configured tolerance without overwriting any radiative flux channel.

Surface-layer turbulence and boundary-layer tracer transport

Monin-Obukhov similarity and aerodynamic resistance

relic.scientific.atmosphere.turbulence.TurbulenceFunctor (Level 3) closes constant-flux surface-layer momentum and scalar exchange using Monin-Obukhov similarity theory (Monin and Obukhov, 1954; Businger et al., 1971; Dyer, 1974). For reference height \(z_r\), displacement height \(d_0\), aerodynamic and thermal roughness lengths \(z_{0m}, z_{0h}\), von Karman constant \(\kappa\), and virtual potential temperature \(\theta_v\), the Obukhov length \(L\) and dimensionless stability parameter \(\zeta = (z_r - d_0)/L\) satisfy

\[ L = -\frac{\rho_a c_p \theta_v u_\ast^3}{\kappa g H_v}, \qquad u_\ast = \frac{\kappa U(z_r)}{\ln\!\left(\frac{z_r - d_0}{z_{0m}}\right) - \Psi_m(\zeta) + \Psi_m\!\left(\frac{z_{0m}}{L}\right)}, \]
\[ r_a = \frac{\ln\!\left(\frac{z_r - d_0}{z_{0h}}\right) - \Psi_h(\zeta) + \Psi_h\!\left(\frac{z_{0h}}{L}\right)}{\kappa u_\ast}, \qquad K_m(z) = \frac{\kappa u_\ast (z - d_0)}{\phi_m(\zeta)}, \qquad K_h(z) = \frac{\kappa u_\ast (z - d_0)}{\phi_h(\zeta)}, \]

where \(\phi_m, \phi_h\) and their integrated stability corrections \(\Psi_m, \Psi_h\) follow Businger-Dyer unstable (\(\zeta < 0\)) and linear stable (\(\zeta \ge 0\)) branches. Sensible and latent heat fluxes \(H = \rho_a c_p (\theta_s - \theta_a)/r_a\) and \(\lambda E\) are emitted as instantaneous REPLACE boundary fields.

Vertical K-profile and 3D advection-diffusion transport

relic.scientific.atmosphere.transport.eddy_diffusion_blm.EddyDiffusionBLMFunctor (Level 3) advances 1D vertical boundary-layer tracer concentrations \(c(z,t)\) across a boundary layer of height \(h_{\mathrm{BL}}\) with profile diffusivity (Troen and Mahrt, 1986; Holtslag and Boville, 1993):

\[ K_z(z) = \kappa\,w_s\,z\left(1 - \frac{z}{h_{\mathrm{BL}}}\right)^p, \qquad 0 \le z \le h_{\mathrm{BL}}, \]

discretized in conservative finite-volume cochain form

\[ \frac{\mathrm{d}c_h}{\mathrm{d}t} = -D_h q_h + G_h u_h, \qquad q_h = -\star_{K_z} d_h c_h, \qquad \star_{K_z} = \star_{K_z}^{\mathsf{T}} \succeq 0, \]

where surface emissions and dry-deposition sinks enter only through the boundary port \(G_h u_h\).

relic.scientific.atmosphere.transport.AtmosphericAdvectionDiffusionFunctor.AtmosphericAdvectionDiffusionFunctor (Level 3) generalizes tracer transport to three-dimensional structured grids with wind components \((u, v, w)\) and anisotropic eddy diffusivities \(\mathbf{K} = \operatorname{diag}(K_x, K_y, K_z)\):

\[ \frac{\partial c}{\partial t} + \nabla \cdot (\mathbf{v} c - \mathbf{K}\nabla c) = s_{\mathrm{ext}}, \]

using conservative upwind advective face fluxes and centered diffusive face fluxes. Both transport Functors emit integrated per-step concentration increments via UpdateItem(ACCUMULATE, per_timestep=True) and verify discrete column or volume mass closure against boundary fluxes.

Environmental forcing projection and Wesely dry deposition

relic.scientific.atmosphere.environmental_forcing.EnvironmentalForcingFunctor (Level 2) validates physical bounds, unit consistency, and finiteness of prescribed meteorological and trace-gas driver arrays before projecting them into StateManager via REPLACE.

relic.scientific.atmosphere.transport.dry_deposition_aggregator.DryDepositionAggregator (Level 3) computes species-resolved dry-deposition velocities \(v_{d,s}\) [m s\(^{-1}\)] and downward surface removal fluxes \(F_{\mathrm{dep},s} = v_{d,s} c_s(z_r)\) using the Wesely (1989) series-parallel resistance network (aerodynamic resistance \(r_a\), quasi-laminar sublayer resistance \(r_{b,s} = \frac{2}{\kappa u_\ast}(\mathrm{Sc}_s / \mathrm{Pr})^{2/3}\), and bulk canopy/surface resistance \(r_{c,s}\) combining stomatal, cuticular, mesophyll, and ground pathways in parallel):

\[ v_{d,s} = \frac{1}{r_a + r_{b,s} + r_{c,s}} + v_{\mathrm{grav},s}, \qquad F_{\mathrm{dep},s} = v_{d,s}\,c_s(z_r) \ge 0. \]

Because \(r_a, r_{b,s}, r_{c,s} > 0\), conductance \(v_{d,s}\) is strictly positive and defines a passive linear boundary sink (\(\star_{v_d} \succeq 0\)) without directly mutating atmospheric concentration storage; the selected atmospheric transport owner consumes \(F_{\mathrm{dep},s}\) once.

Gas-phase chemistry: open tropospheric kinetics and closed Level-5 GENERIC point

Open tropospheric photostationary and BVOC-HOx mode (Level 3)

In its default open-atmosphere configuration, relic.scientific.atmosphere.chemistry.ChemistryFunctor (Level 3) evaluates tropospheric \(\mathrm{NO}\)-\(\mathrm{NO}_2\)-\(\mathrm{O}_3\) photostationary kinetics coupled to biogenic VOC (\(\mathrm{C_5H_8}\), monoterpene, and sesquiterpene) oxidation by \(\mathrm{OH}\), \(\mathrm{O}_3\), and \(\mathrm{NO}_3\) (Methods of Ecological Analysis, Chapter 18; Leighton, 1961; Atkinson et al., 2006; Warneck and Williams, 2012). Key photolysis and thermal channels include:

\[ \mathrm{NO}_2 + h\nu \xrightarrow{J_{\mathrm{NO}_2}} \mathrm{NO} + \mathrm{O}(^3\mathrm{P}), \qquad \mathrm{O}(^3\mathrm{P}) + \mathrm{O}_2 + \mathrm{M} \longrightarrow \mathrm{O}_3 + \mathrm{M}, \qquad \mathrm{NO} + \mathrm{O}_3 \xrightarrow{k_3(T)} \mathrm{NO}_2 + \mathrm{O}_2, \]

with Arrhenius rate coefficient \(k_3(T) = A_3 \exp(-E_3 / (R T))\) and spectral quantum-yield temperature interpolation for \(J_{\mathrm{NO}_2}\) and \(J_{\mathrm{O}(^1\mathrm{D})}\). In photostationary equilibrium without peroxy radical perturbation, the Leighton ratio satisfies

\[ \Phi_{\mathrm{PSS}} = \frac{J_{\mathrm{NO}_2}[\mathrm{NO}_2]}{k_3(T)[\mathrm{NO}][\mathrm{O}_3]} = 1, \qquad [\mathrm{O}_3]_{\mathrm{PSS}} = \frac{J_{\mathrm{NO}_2}[\mathrm{NO}_2]}{k_3(T)[\mathrm{NO}]}. \]

Because actinic photon driving (\(J_{\mathrm{NO}_2} > 0\)), external BVOC sources, and open boundary conditions supply external free energy, the default mode is certified at Level 3 (stoichiometric reaction-operator factorization \(\dot{\mathbf{c}} = \mathbf{N}\mathbf{r}(\mathbf{c}, T, J)\) with verified \(\mathrm{NO}_x\) and oxidant accounting, without claiming closed thermodynamic GENERIC degeneracy).

Closed constant-volume adiabatic GENERIC runtime point (Level 5)

Under the secondary constructor-conditioned certificate constructor_selection = {physical_boundary: "closed_constant_volume"} (ChemistryFunctor closed constant-volume, Level 5), ChemistryFunctor restricts execution to an isolated, rigid, constant-volume (\(V\) = box_volume [m\(^3\)]) adiabatic gas cell containing dark thermal titration

\[ \mathrm{NO} + \mathrm{O}_3 \xrightarrow{k_3(T)} \mathrm{NO}_2 + \mathrm{O}_2 \]

with zero actinic flux (\(J = 0\)) and zero matter or heat ports. Let \(\mathbf{x} = (n_{\mathrm{NO}}, n_{\mathrm{NO}_2}, n_{\mathrm{O}_3}, n_{\mathrm{O}_2}, U)^{\mathsf{T}}\) denote the molar inventories \(\mathbf{n}\) [mol] (box_no_amount, box_no2_amount, box_o3_amount, box_o2_amount) and total NASA7 internal energy \(U\) [J] (box_internal_energy), with extended stoichiometric vector \(\tilde{\boldsymbol{\nu}} = (\boldsymbol{\nu}^{\mathsf{T}}, 0)^{\mathsf{T}} = (-1, +1, -1, +1, 0)^{\mathsf{T}}\). On the strictly positive thermodynamic interior (\(n_i > 0\), \(T > 0\), partial pressures \(p_i = n_i R T / V\), NASA7 chemical potentials \(\mu_i = h_i(T) - T s_i(T) + R T \ln(p_i / p_0)\)), the energy functional \(E(\mathbf{x}) = U\) and concave entropy \(S(\mathbf{n}, U)\) yield conjugate effort covectors

\[ \mathbf{e}_E = \frac{\partial E}{\partial \mathbf{x}} = (\mathbf{0}^{\mathsf{T}}, 1)^{\mathsf{T}}, \qquad \mathbf{e}_S = \frac{\partial S}{\partial \mathbf{x}} = \left(-\frac{\boldsymbol{\mu}(\mathbf{n}, T)}{T}^{\mathsf{T}},\; \frac{1}{T}\right)^{\mathsf{T}}, \]

with positive reaction affinity \(\mathcal{A} = -\boldsymbol{\nu}^{\mathsf{T}}\boldsymbol{\mu} > 0\) and forward molar rate \(r > 0\) (Grmela and Ottinger, 1997; Mielke, 2011). Advancement through relic.computation.operators.iphs.advance_reaction_network satisfies the canonical GENERIC evolution (\(L_h = 0\))

\[ \frac{\mathrm{d}\mathbf{x}}{\mathrm{d}t} = L_h\mathbf{e}_E + M_h\mathbf{e}_S = \tilde{\boldsymbol{\nu}}\,r, \qquad M_h(\mathbf{x}) = \frac{r\,T}{\mathcal{A}}\,\tilde{\boldsymbol{\nu}}\,\tilde{\boldsymbol{\nu}}^{\mathsf{T}} \succeq 0, \]

with exact energy degeneracy \(M_h(\mathbf{x})\mathbf{e}_E = \mathbf{0}\) (\(\Delta U = 0\)), exact atomic nitrogen and oxygen conservation (\(\Delta(n_{\mathrm{NO}} + n_{\mathrm{NO}_2}) = 0\), \(\Delta(n_{\mathrm{NO}} + 2n_{\mathrm{NO}_2} + 3n_{\mathrm{O}_3} + 2n_{\mathrm{O}_2}) = 0\)), and non-negative entropy production \(\sigma_S = \mathbf{e}_S^{\mathsf{T}} M_h(\mathbf{x})\mathbf{e}_S = \mathcal{A} r / T \ge 0\). This Level 5 certificate applies exclusively to the closed constant-volume configuration and never transfers to photolytic or open-boundary runs.

Stochastic atmospheric forcing and replay contracts

Both stochastic weather Functors inherit relic.core.functor.stochastic_substream.FunctorSubstreamMixin ([I]), binding an isolated PyTorch Generator substream whose byte state is persisted across checkpoint and restart so trajectories replay bit-identically (Methods of Ecological Analysis, Chapter 27). Step durations are converted to days via \(\Delta t_{\mathrm{days}} = \Delta t / 86400\).

Ornstein-Uhlenbeck air-temperature anomaly

relic.scientific.atmosphere.stochastic_temperature.OrnsteinUhlenbeckTemperatureFunctor (Level 2) models mean-reverting synoptic temperature anomalies \(X_t\) [K] via the Ito stochastic differential equation

\[ \mathrm{d}X_t = -\alpha X_t\,\mathrm{d}t_{\mathrm{days}} + \sigma\,\mathrm{d}W_{t_{\mathrm{days}}}, \qquad \alpha > 0,\quad \sigma \ge 0, \]

discretized over \(\Delta t_{\mathrm{days}}\) by relic.stochastic.sde_solver.EulerMaruyamaSolver:

\[ \Delta X_n = -\alpha X_n\,\Delta t_{\mathrm{days}} + \sigma\sqrt{\Delta t_{\mathrm{days}}}\,\xi_n, \qquad \xi_n \sim \mathcal{N}(0, 1). \]

stochastic_air_temperature_anomaly is updated via UpdateItem(ACCUMULATE, per_timestep=True), while step drift, diffusion scale, and Wiener increments are emitted as REPLACE diagnostics.

Marked Poisson storm rainfall generator

relic.scientific.atmosphere.stochastic_rainfall.MarkedPoissonRainfallFunctor (Level 2) generates intermittent storm arrivals of constant hazard rate \(\lambda > 0\) [day\(^{-1}\)] and independent exponential storm depths of mean \(\alpha > 0\) [mm] (Rodriguez-Iturbe et al., 1999). Exact inter-arrival sampling over \(\Delta t_{\mathrm{days}}\) is performed by relic.stochastic.gillespie.GillespieSSA subject to a fail-closed per-cell arrival cap \(K_{\max} = \lceil \mu + 12\sqrt{\mu} + 64 \rceil\) (\(\mu = \lambda \Delta t_{\mathrm{days}}\)). For \(k = \Delta N_n\) arrivals with unit exponential variates \(E_i \sim \operatorname{Exp}(1)\) (reparameterized as \(z_i = \alpha E_i\) so pathwise \(\partial \Delta H_n / \partial \alpha\) preserves PyTorch autograd), the cumulative event count and rainfall depth increments are

\[ \Delta N_n = k, \qquad \Delta H_n = \alpha \sum_{i=1}^{k} E_i, \]

with predictable Doob-Meyer compensators

\[ \widetilde{A}_{N,n} = \lambda\,\Delta t_{\mathrm{days}}, \qquad \widetilde{A}_{H,n} = \lambda\,\alpha\,\Delta t_{\mathrm{days}}, \]

emitted in diagnostics.stochastic_rainfall.compensator_count and diagnostics.stochastic_rainfall.compensator_depth so martingale innovation residuals can be audited directly.

Atmospheric observation operators

Read-only atmospheric observation Functors live in relic.coupling.observation outside the 90 certified relic.scientific.* process classes. All five inherit ScaleCouplingFunctor at ExecutionPhase.POSTPROCESSING and emit VariableKind.DIAGNOSTIC outputs with StateWriteMode.DERIVED (\(\Delta \mathbf{S} = 0\)):

  1. Steady-state HCHO column yield (HchoSteadyStateColumnOperator): relic.coupling.observation.hcho_steady_state_column.HchoSteadyStateColumnOperator maps surface isoprene molar emission \(E\) [mol m\(^{-2}\) s\(^{-1}\)] (with stoichiometric carbon factor \(n_C = 5\)) and tropospheric \(\mathrm{NO}_2\) vertical column \(\Omega_{\mathrm{NO}_2}\) [molecule cm\(^{-2}\)] to steady-state formaldehyde vertical column density \(\Omega_{\mathrm{HCHO}}\) (Palmer et al., 2003; Marais et al., 2012):

    \[\begin{split}\Omega_{\mathrm{HCHO}} = S\!\left(\Omega_{\mathrm{NO}_2}\right) n_C E_{\mathrm{molec}} + B, \qquad S\!\left(\Omega_{\mathrm{NO}_2}\right) = \begin{cases} m\,\Omega_{\mathrm{NO}_2} + c, & \Omega_{\mathrm{NO}_2} \le \Omega_t,\\ S_{\mathrm{high}}, & \Omega_{\mathrm{NO}_2} > \Omega_t, \end{cases}\end{split}\]

    where \(E_{\mathrm{molec}}\) converts mol m\(^{-2}\) s\(^{-1}\) to molecule cm\(^{-2}\) s\(^{-1}\) via avogadro, \(\Omega_t = 1.0 \times 10^{15}\) molecule cm\(^{-2}\), and \(B\) is background column density. Inputs below the empirical calibration bound \(\Omega_{\mathrm{low}} = 2.0 \times 10^{14}\) molecule cm\(^{-2}\) are evaluated unclipped along the linear branch while flagging no2_below_calibrated_range = True.

  2. Satellite averaging-kernel column projection (SatelliteColumnKernelOperator): relic.coupling.observation.satellite_column_kernel.SatelliteColumnKernelOperator constructs hybrid-sigma pressure centers \(p_k = a_k + b_k p_s\) and arithmetic or logarithmic interior layer edges (\(p_{e,k} = \frac{1}{2}(p_{k-1}+p_k)\) or \(\sqrt{p_{k-1}p_k}\)), computes hydrostatic dry-air and tracer partial columns

    \[n_k = \frac{p_{e,k} - p_{e,k+1}}{g\,M_{\mathrm{air}}}, \qquad x_{\mathrm{pc},k} = \chi_k\,n_k,\]

    conservatively remaps model partial columns onto retrieval pressure layers via 1D pressure-interval overlap (remap_partial_columns, recording any out-of-range mass in unmapped_column), and applies the retrieval averaging kernel \(A_l\) across the target vertical domain \(T\) (Rodgers, 2000; Eskes and Boersma, 2003):

    \[N_{v,\mathrm{model}} = \sum_{l \in T} A_l\,x_{\mathrm{pc},l}^{\mathrm{retr}}.\]

  3. Tower footprint, profile, and post-observation quality operators: relic.coupling.observation.tower_flux.TowerFluxSupportOperator projects gridded surface fluxes onto normalized eddy-covariance footprint weights; relic.coupling.observation.tower_concentration.TowerConcentrationProfileOperator interpolates vertical mixing-ratio profiles to tower intake heights; and relic.coupling.observation.quality.PostObservationQualityOperator evaluates retrieval validity masks and support-coverage flags without altering model prognostic state.
