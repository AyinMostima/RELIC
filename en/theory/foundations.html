Mathematical Foundations, State, Units, and Conservation

Architectural contract and notation

The Recomposable Ecosystem Library for Integrated Coupling (RELIC) represents a terrestrial biogeochemical model as a directed composition of typed scientific Functors under shared parameter and state governance (Methods of Ecological Analysis, Chapters 1–5, 10, 11, and 14). Each concrete Functor maps declared read-only state and external forcing tensors to typed update records without mutating shared storage in place:

\[ \mathcal{F}_{\boldsymbol{\theta}_i}: (\mathbf{S}_t,\; \boldsymbol{\xi}_t,\; t,\; \Delta t) \longmapsto \{\Delta \mathbf{S}_i,\; \mathbf{q}_i,\; \mathbf{d}_i\}, \]

where \(\mathbf{S}_t\) is the prognostic state vector, \(\boldsymbol{\xi}_t\) is external forcing, \(\boldsymbol{\theta}_i\) is the ParameterManager-owned parameter vector, \(\mathbf{q}_i\) denotes flux or tendency outputs, and \(\mathbf{d}_i\) denotes diagnostic observations. RELICSimulator (relic.core.simulator) governs topological execution and multi-rate scheduling, while StateManager (relic.core.state.manager) executes unit validation, bounds accounting, elemental conservation ledgers, and atomic commits.

Spatial quantity basis and temporal overlap laws

Scale labels do not define cell geometry, cell areas, or extensivity. Basis-aware spatial operators (ConservativeRemap, ConservativeRegridder, SpatialAggregation, HierarchicalAggregator, ScaleManager, simulator auto-regridding, and external-forcing adapters) require an explicit SpatialQuantityBasis (relic.coupling.scales.types) via spatial_basis metadata or the quantity_basis argument (Methods of Ecological Analysis, Chapters 2 and 14):

Bare "extensive" is rejected with ValueError because it fails to distinguish per-cell totals from areal densities. Exact conversion between cell_total and area_density (convert_spatial_basis) requires strictly positive cell areas \(A_i > 0\) (\(X_i = x_i A_i\)); conversions involving intensive or categorical raise ValueError. Note that SpatialQuantityBasis governs spatial support transfer and is distinct from CochainSpaceSpec.extensivity in relic.computation.operators.dec.forms.

For first-order overlap remapping with source-target overlap area \(A_{iJ} \ge 0\), source area \(A_i > 0\), target area \(A_J > 0\), covered target area \(C_J = \sum_i A_{iJ}\), and covered source fraction \(f_i = \sum_J A_{iJ}/A_i \le 1\), ConservativeRemap and ConservativeRegridder evaluate:

\[ Y_J = \sum_i X_i\,\frac{A_{iJ}}{A_i} \quad (\texttt{cell\_total}), \qquad y_J = \frac{1}{A_J}\sum_i x_i A_{iJ} \quad (\texttt{area\_density}), \qquad y_J = \frac{1}{C_J}\sum_i x_i A_{iJ} \quad (\texttt{intensive}). \]

Amounts are conserved strictly over covered support:

\[ \sum_J Y_J = \sum_i f_i X_i, \qquad U = \sum_i (1 - f_i)\,X_i, \]

where uncovered source mass \(U\) is reported in diagnostics and never rescaled onto covered target cells. Explicit cell bounds, overlap matrices, or polygon vertices are mandatory; bounds are never inferred from coordinate spacing. External-model ingestion reads Climate and Forecast (CF) bounds for 1D rectilinear coordinates and rejects 2D curvilinear grids lacking explicit cell corners during conservative remapping (SpatialScaling.regrid with method="conservative" on a coordinate-free grid descriptor switches to non-conservative nearest-neighbour lookup).

In time, TemporalAggregator and TemporalDisaggregator (relic.coupling.scales.temporal) require explicit temporal_semantics (interval_mean, rate, interval_accumulation, or state_at_boundary; point_sample and categorical are rejected) together with explicit source and target units. Over target interval \(j\) of duration \(\tau_j\) with source-target overlap durations \(O_{ij}\) and validity mask \(m_i \in \{0, 1\}\), interval_mean and rate-to-rate mappings evaluate:

\[ \bar{x}_j = \frac{\sum_i m_i O_{ij}\,x_i}{\sum_i m_i O_{ij}}, \qquad c_j = \frac{1}{\tau_j}\sum_i m_i O_{ij}, \]

returning \(\bar{x}_j\) when dimensionless coverage \(c_j\) meets coupling.scales.temporal.coverage_threshold and NaN below threshold without extrapolating missing intervals. TemporalScaling maps fixed SI durations via method (mean, rate, sum, total, extensive, or law names) and rejects calendar-dependent month or year intervals.

Universal conservation and balance laws

Local, stoichiometric, and control-volume balances

For a conserved scalar density \(c(\mathbf{x}, t)\) with velocity \(\mathbf{v}\), diffusivity \(D\), internal stoichiometric network \(N r(c, \boldsymbol{\theta}, \boldsymbol{\xi})\), and open-boundary exchange \(s_{\mathrm{bnd}}\), the continuum and integral control-volume balances on domain \(\Omega\) with outward normal \(\mathbf{n}\) are (Methods of Ecological Analysis, Chapters 3–5):

\[ \frac{\partial c}{\partial t} + \nabla \cdot \left(\mathbf{v}c - D\nabla c\right) = N r + s_{\mathrm{bnd}}, \qquad \frac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}t}\int_{\Omega} c\,\mathrm{d}V = -\int_{\partial\Omega} \mathbf{F}\cdot\mathbf{n}\,\mathrm{d}A + \int_{\Omega} N r\,\mathrm{d}V + \int_{\Omega} s_{\mathrm{bnd}}\,\mathrm{d}V. \]

After spatial discretization into prognostic reservoir vector \(\mathbf{S}(t) \in \mathbb{R}^n\) with internal process stoichiometries \(\{\mathbf{N}_p\}_{p \in \mathcal{P}_{\mathrm{int}}}\) and boundary port operators \(\{\mathbf{B}_q\}_{q \in \mathcal{P}_{\mathrm{ext}}}\), the semi-discrete coupled evolution is:

\[ \frac{\mathrm{d}\mathbf{S}}{\mathrm{d}t} = \sum_{p \in \mathcal{P}_{\mathrm{int}}} \mathbf{N}_p\,\mathbf{J}_p(\mathbf{S}, \boldsymbol{\xi}, \boldsymbol{\theta}, t) + \sum_{q \in \mathcal{P}_{\mathrm{ext}}} \mathbf{B}_q\,\mathbf{u}_q(\mathbf{S}, \boldsymbol{\xi}, \boldsymbol{\theta}, t). \]

For any conserved elemental ledger matrix \(C\) (rows encoding water, carbon, nitrogen, phosphorus, or charge content per state unit), internal closure requires the stoichiometric nullspace condition:

\[ C \mathbf{N}_p = 0 \quad \forall p \in \mathcal{P}_{\mathrm{int}} \qquad \Longrightarrow \qquad \frac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}t}(C\mathbf{S}) = \sum_{q \in \mathcal{P}_{\mathrm{ext}}} C\mathbf{B}_q\,\mathbf{u}_q. \]

Non-zero changes in \(C\mathbf{S}\) must traverse an explicit port, boundary export, managed export, diagnostic residual, or observation route.

Energy and entropy balances

When a process declares thermodynamic energy \(E(\mathbf{S})\), entropy \(S(\mathbf{S})\), and conjugate port effort-flow pairs \((e_{\mathrm{port}}, f_{\mathrm{port}})\) with port power \(P_{\mathrm{port}} = e_{\mathrm{port}}^{\mathsf{T}} f_{\mathrm{port}}\), the first- and second-law balances take the form:

\[ \frac{\mathrm{d}E}{\mathrm{d}t} = P_{\mathrm{in}} - P_{\mathrm{out}} - D_E, \quad D_E \ge 0, \qquad \frac{\mathrm{d}S}{\mathrm{d}t} = \Phi_S + \sigma, \quad \sigma \ge 0, \]

where \(D_E\) is internal mechanical/thermal dissipation, \(\Phi_S\) is boundary entropy flux, and \(\sigma\) is internal entropy production. Empirical rate laws without declared potentials and operators are audited through mass/energy budget ledgers at certificate Levels 1–3 without fabricating synthetic thermodynamic potentials.

Transactional StateManager governance (I1–I7)

partition_outputs (relic.core.state.types) splits every Functor return mapping into state updates (UpdateItem), diagnostics, and immutable ExternalIntent records according to UpdateMode (ACCUMULATE, REPLACE, FLUX) and StateWriteMode (MUTABLE, IMMUTABLE, DERIVED):

\[ \mathbf{S}_{n+1} = \mathbf{S}_n + \Delta t\,\mathbf{q} \quad (\texttt{ACCUMULATE}/\texttt{FLUX},\; \texttt{per\_timestep=False}), \qquad \mathbf{S}_{n+1} = \mathbf{S}_n + \Delta\mathbf{S} \quad (\texttt{ACCUMULATE},\; \texttt{per\_timestep=True}), \qquad \mathbf{S}_{n+1} = \mathbf{S}_{\mathrm{new}} \quad (\texttt{REPLACE}). \]

Centralized ParameterManager and physical-constant SSOT

ParameterManager (relic.parameters.manager) owns all model coefficients, bounds, units, dtypes, references, and gradient flags. In the live database under relic/parameters/database/, 138 YAML files (134 under relic/ and 4 under calibrated/) define 4,751 leaf entries (4,642 core + 109 calibrated overrides), resolving to 4,643 active default runtime parameters of which 810 carry with_gradients: true for differentiable calibration. Inside a Functor, parameters are retrieved exclusively via self.get_parameter_value(name, required=...).

Universal and material physical constants (CODATA gas constant \(R\), Stefan-Boltzmann constant \(\sigma\), Avogadro constant \(N_A\), standard gravity \(g\), von Karman constant \(\kappa\), molar masses, and phase-change enthalpies) reside solely in relic/parameters/database/relic/physical/constants.yaml as a Single Source of Truth (SSOT) and are accessed via get_physical_constant (relic.utils.physical_constants). Domain YAML files and Python modules never redefine physical constants. Dimensional conversions preserve exact stoichiometry; converting molar assimilation \(A\) [\(\mu\)mol CO\(_2\) m\(^{-2}\) s\(^{-1}\)] to carbon mass flux \(F_{\mathrm{C}}\) [kg C m\(^{-2}\) s\(^{-1}\)] applies \(F_{\mathrm{C}} = A\,M_{\mathrm{C}}\,10^{-9}\) using SSOT carbon molar mass \(M_{\mathrm{C}}\) [g mol\(^{-1}\)].

Piecewise-smooth discrete adjoint differentiation and device policy

Because FunctorBase subclasses torch.nn.Module, macro-step transitions \(\mathbf{S}_t = \Phi_t(\mathbf{S}_{t-1}, \boldsymbol{\theta})\) (\(t = 1, \dots, N_t\)) from initial state \(\mathbf{S}_0(\boldsymbol{\theta})\) construct an exact reverse-mode discrete adjoint graph in torch.autograd (Methods of Ecological Analysis, Chapter 10). Because \(\boldsymbol{\theta}\) enters every step transition \(\Phi_t\) directly, the total terminal objective gradient \(\frac{\mathrm{d}\mathcal{L}(\mathbf{S}_{N_t}, \boldsymbol{\theta})}{\mathrm{d}\boldsymbol{\theta}}\) accumulates step-wise parameter Jacobians across the full trajectory:

\[ \frac{\mathrm{d}\mathcal{L}}{\mathrm{d}\boldsymbol{\theta}} = \frac{\partial \mathcal{L}}{\partial \boldsymbol{\theta}} + \frac{\partial \mathcal{L}}{\partial \mathbf{S}_{N_t}} \left(\prod_{k=1}^{N_t} \frac{\partial \Phi_k}{\partial \mathbf{S}_{k-1}}\right) \frac{\partial \mathbf{S}_0}{\partial \boldsymbol{\theta}} + \sum_{t=1}^{N_t} \frac{\partial \mathcal{L}}{\partial \mathbf{S}_{N_t}} \left(\prod_{k=t+1}^{N_t} \frac{\partial \Phi_k}{\partial \mathbf{S}_{k-1}}\right) \frac{\partial \Phi_t}{\partial \boldsymbol{\theta}}, \]

where matrix products are ordered from left (\(k = N_t\)) to right (\(k = 1\) or \(k = t + 1\)) and the empty product at \(t = N_t\) equals the identity matrix \(\mathbf{I}_n\). Smooth PyTorch tensor paths, linear overlap remaps without post-clipping, and implicit scalar roots solved via BracketedBisectionSolver (using implicit-function differentiation) propagate exact discrete sensitivities. Transversal guard crossings require explicit saltation-matrix factors (relic.computation.solvers.hybrid_events; see Numerical Integration, Splitting, Stability, and Stochastic Processes), whereas hard bounds clipping, discrete regime switches, adaptive step-size rejections, and NumPy-based external ingestion regridding act as non-differentiable boundaries.

Device placement is governed centrally by relic.core.device_policy (device, require_gpu, device_overrides, module_device_overrides). FunctorBase.__init__ allocates CPU placeholders, and simulator registration migrates modules to the resolved target device (GPU-first with automatic CPU fallback unless require_gpu: true or an unsatisfied enable_distributed: true contract raises). Scientific modules never invoke torch.cuda.is_available() directly.

Bounded mathematical contracts and runtime boundary ([I] vs [N])

Three decidable finite-dimensional operator contracts in relic.computation.operators provide numerical verification against validation/iphs.yaml tolerances (Methods of Ecological Analysis, Chapters 1–3 and 10):
